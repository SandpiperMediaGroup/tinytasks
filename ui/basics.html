TinyTasks basics

My Desk is for today

Put the tasks you want to focus on here. They stay in their original lists. Your desk clears each day; your tasks do not.

Desk times are a plan for when to work, separate from due dates. Add a start and an optional duration, or leave a task untimed. Clear time keeps it on your desk.

Ask your agent

Try “Plan my day in TinyTasks” or “Add the next steps to my Studio list.” An agent can add tasks and suggest a timed desk for you to accept or decline.

Agents can also ask a question in task details. Choose an answer and send it for the agent to pick up.

Lists keep related work together

A list holds tasks, with optional subtasks for smaller steps. Use Personal for loose tasks. Create a list with the + beside Lists & Chats, or right-click the sidebar and choose New list.

Right-click a named list to rename, reorder, archive, or delete it. Archive empty lists quietly tidies up lists that contain no tasks.

Inside a task row

Check it off when done. Click the row to open details, or click its text to edit. Link chips open directly. The three-dot menu has due date, priority, list, and desk actions.

Add subtasks for steps, switch between numbered steps and a checklist, and drag to reorder them. A lamp appears on hover when the task is on My Desk.
